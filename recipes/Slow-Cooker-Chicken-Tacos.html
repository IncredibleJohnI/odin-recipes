Slow Cooker Chicken Tacos

Description

A quick, family-favorite recipe for classic ground beef tacos. Packed with flavor and perfectly customizable with your favorite toppings like salsa, sour cream, and fresh guacamole.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground chicken
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 8 hard taco shells (or small flour tortillas)
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 large tomato, diced

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat until completely cooked through, about 6 to 8 minutes. Drain excess grease.
  2. Reduce heat to medium. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, onion powder, garlic powder, tomato sauce, and 1/4 cup of water.
  3. Simmer for 5 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the meat evenly.
  4. While the meat simmers, warm your taco shells in the oven according to the package directions.
  5. Assemble the tacos by spooning 2-3 tablespoons of the beef mixture into each shell, then top with lettuce, tomatoes, and shredded cheese.
