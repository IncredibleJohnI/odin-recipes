Chicken Tacos

Description

These chicken tacos are a delicious and easy way to enjoy flavorful, tender chicken in a quick and convenient manner.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground chicken
  • 1 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 8 hard taco shells (or small flour tortillas)
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 large tomato, diced

Steps

  1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the ground chicken until completely cooked through, about 6 to 8 minutes. Drain excess grease.
  2. Reduce heat to medium. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, onion powder, garlic powder, tomato sauce, and 1/4 cup of water.
  3. Simmer for 5 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken evenly.
  4. While the chicken simmers, warm your taco shells in the oven according to the package directions.
  5. Assemble the tacos by spooning 2-3 tablespoons of the chicken mixture into each shell, then top with lettuce, tomatoes, and shredded cheese.
