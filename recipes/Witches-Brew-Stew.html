Witches' Brew Stew

Description

A spooktacular, hearty autumn stew packed with tender chunks of beef, vibrant root vegetables, and a deep, savory broth that tastes like pure magic on a cold evening.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs beef chuck roast, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 large carrots, thickly sliced
  • 3 russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup red wine (or extra beef broth)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Season the beef cubes generously with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the beef to the pot and sear until browned on all sides, then remove the meat and set it aside on a plate.
  3. In the same pot, add the chopped onion and garlic. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the onion becomes soft and translucent.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, dried thyme, beef broth, and red wine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
  5. Return the beef and its juices to the pot. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 1 hour.
  6. Stir in the carrots and potatoes. Cover and continue to simmer for another 45 minutes, or until the vegetables and beef are completely tender.
